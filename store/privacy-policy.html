Vidya Nova — Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date]

Vidya Nova ("the app") is a school-management app operated by [your company / your name] ("we", "us"). Schools use Vidya Nova to share attendance, homework, notices, timetables, fees, exam results and other school information with parents, students and staff. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Who can use the app

Accounts are created by the school, not in the app. Parents/guardians sign in with the account the school gives them; teachers and staff sign in with their staff account. The app is meant to be used by parents/guardians and school staff.

Data we process

  • Account details: school code, username, name, role, email or phone number provided by the school.
  • Student records provided by the school: name, class, photo, attendance, homework, exam marks and report cards, fee records, teacher remarks, transport details.
  • Staff records (staff accounts): attendance check-in/out, timetable, leave requests and similar.
  • Files you upload (staff): photos and PDFs such as notices, syllabus or homework attachments.
  • Device data: a random app-install ID (used to protect sign-in against abuse) and a push-notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) so the school can send you notifications.

We do not use the data for advertising, we do not sell it, and the app contains no ads or third-party analytics.

Why we use it

Only to provide the service to the school: showing each user the information the school shares with them, sending school notifications, and keeping accounts secure.

Sharing

Data is visible only to the school that owns it and the users that school authorises. We use service providers to run the app: cloud hosting and database ([hosting provider, e.g. Oracle Cloud / Render], [database, e.g. TiDB Cloud]) and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications. They process data only on our behalf.

Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in tokens are stored in the phone's secure keystore and personal data cached on the phone is encrypted.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the school uses Vidya Nova. To correct or delete your data or close your account, contact your school or write to us at [support email]; we act on verified requests within [30] days. When a school stops using the service, its data is deleted within [90] days.

Children

Student information is entered and managed by the school. The app is used by parents/guardians on behalf of their children and by school staff.

Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
